# About the Public Interest Tech Library

The Public Interest Tech Library is a curated, browsable catalog of **free
learning resources** for public sector professionals — the people building,
buying, and governing the digital services communities rely on.

Every entry is tagged by topic, skill level, and format so you can quickly find
material that fits where you are and what you need to do next. Topics span
artificial intelligence, data, digital services, procurement, accessibility,
civic engagement, and policy.

All resources listed here are freely available from their providers. Follow the
link on each entry to reach the original source.
