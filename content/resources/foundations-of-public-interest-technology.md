Public interest technology sits at the crossroads of technology and the public
good. This course introduces the field, its history, and the roles
practitioners play inside government, nonprofits, and advocacy organizations.

You'll explore case studies where thoughtful technology decisions expanded
access to benefits, and where poor ones caused harm. By the end you'll be able
to describe the core values of the field and identify entry points for your own
career.
