As agencies adopt AI for everything from fraud detection to service triage,
governance becomes essential. This guide covers the OECD AI Principles, risk
classification, transparency obligations, and accountability structures.

It is aimed at policy leads and senior technologists who must design procurement
rules, impact assessments, and oversight processes that keep automated systems
fair, contestable, and aligned with public values.
