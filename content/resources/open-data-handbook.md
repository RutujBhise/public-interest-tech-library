Open data lets citizens, journalists, and businesses build on the information
governments already hold. This handbook explains what makes data "open," the
value it creates, and how to publish it responsibly.

It walks through choosing datasets, applying open licenses, selecting formats,
and engaging a community of reusers so that published data actually gets used.
