Accessibility is a legal requirement and an equity imperative for government
services. This guide explains Section 508, the WCAG standards it references, and
the responsibilities of designers, developers, and content authors.

It includes checklists for common tasks — writing alt text, structuring
documents, and testing with a keyboard and screen reader — so you can catch
issues before they reach the public.
