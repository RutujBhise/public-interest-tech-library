The U.S. Web Design System (USWDS) gives teams a shared foundation of
components, design tokens, and patterns that are accessible and mobile-friendly
out of the box.

Use it to ship consistent interfaces faster while meeting Section 508
requirements. The documentation covers implementation, theming, and the
research behind each component so teams can adapt it to their own service.
