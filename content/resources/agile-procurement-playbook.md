Traditional government IT procurement often locks agencies into large, risky
contracts. This playbook shows how to break work into smaller modular
contracts, write outcome-based statements of work, and evaluate vendors on
demonstrated ability.

The toolkit includes sample contract language, evaluation criteria, and market
research techniques drawn from successful federal modernization projects.
