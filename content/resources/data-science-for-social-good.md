This program pairs practical data science skills with the realities of public
sector work: messy administrative data, tight resource constraints, and the
need for interpretable, equitable models.

Through project-based learning you'll practice framing a policy question as a
data problem, building responsible predictive models, and communicating results
to non-technical stakeholders who make the final decisions.
