Human-centered design starts with the people you serve. This course walks
through the three phases of the process — inspiration, ideation, and
implementation — using real community projects as examples.

You'll learn to conduct interviews, synthesize insights, prototype cheaply, and
test ideas before committing resources. The methods apply equally to digital
tools and in-person services.
